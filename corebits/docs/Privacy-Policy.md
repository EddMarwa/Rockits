Last updated: October 26, 2025

## 1. Introduction
At Corebits, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is managed responsibly. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our services. By accessing or using Corebits, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

We collect information to provide and improve our services. Types of information we may collect include:
- Personal information you provide: name, email address, phone number, billing details, and any other information you choose to provide when creating an account or contacting us.
- Identity documents and verification data when you complete identity verification or KYC.
- Usage information: IP address, browser type/version, pages visited, device identifiers, and analytics data.

## 3. How We Collect Information
We collect information when you:
- Register for an account or submit forms on our site.

- Provide documents or information as part of KYC or verification.
- Interact with emails, marketing, or support communications.

## 4. How We Use Your Information
We use collected information to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain our services.
- Verify identity and perform KYC where applicable.
- Communicate updates, security notices, and marketing (you can opt out of promotional communications).
- Improve and personalize our services, including analytics and troubleshooting.
- Comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

## 5. Sharing Your Information
We may share your personal information with:
- Service providers and partners who assist in operating our services (subject to confidentiality obligations).
- Law enforcement, courts or regulators where required by law.

We will not sell or rent your personal information to third parties without your explicit consent.

## 6. Data Security
We employ security measures such as encryption and access controls to protect your information. While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We will notify you of any security incidents affecting your data as required by applicable law.

## 7. Your Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of your data.
- Object to or restrict processing of your data.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@corebits.com. We may ask you to verify your identity before responding.

## 8. Data Retention
We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described and to meet legal obligations. When data is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it.

## 9. International Transfers
If your data is transferred internationally, we will put in place appropriate safeguards (e.g., standard contractual clauses) to protect your personal data in accordance with applicable laws.
