# Know Your Customer (KYC) Policy — Corebits

Last updated: October 26, 2025

## 1. Purpose
Corebits’ KYC policy is designed to verify user identity, prevent fraud, and support compliance with legal and regulatory requirements, including anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing (CTF) obligations.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all users who are required to complete identity verification when using Corebits services. We may require KYC for account creation, withdrawals, or certain transactions.

## 3. Information Collected
To verify identity, we may collect:
- Full name, date of birth, and residential address.
- Nationality.
- Government-issued identification (passport, driver’s license, national ID).
- Utility bills or other proof of address when required.

## 4. Submission & Verification
- Users will submit documents via our secure verification platform.
- Documents must be valid, legible, and not expired.
- Verification processing times may vary; typical turnaround is [e.g., 48–72 hours].

## 5. Ongoing Monitoring
We may perform ongoing transaction monitoring and periodic re-verification to detect suspicious activities.

## 6. Non-Compliance
Failure to complete KYC when required may result in account suspension or termination. Corebits reserves the right to refuse service where KYC requirements are not met.

## 7. Data Protection
All KYC data is stored securely and access is limited to authorized personnel. We will not sell or share KYC data with third parties except where required by law or to trusted service providers under strict confidentiality terms.

## 8. Appeals and Support
If your KYC is denied and you wish to appeal, contact us at info@corebits.com. We will review appeals and communicate next steps.

## 9. Changes to Policy
Corebits may update this KYC policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

## 10. Contact
For KYC questions, contact: info@corebits.com
